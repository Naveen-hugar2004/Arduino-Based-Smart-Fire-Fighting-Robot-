# Advantages and Limitations 

## Advantages
- Early fire detection
- Autonomous operation
- Automatic firefighting action
- Environmental monitoring
- Reduced human exposure to hazardous areas
- Cost-effective and scalable
- Multiple sensors for monitoring

## Limitations
- Suitable mainly for small fires
- Sensor readings can be affected by environmental conditions
- Navigation is limited by the implemented movement method
- Battery capacity limits operating time
- Small water tank limits extinguishing capacity
- Remote monitoring needs additional communication implementation
